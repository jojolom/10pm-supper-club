10 PM
Supper Club
Est. 2025

Support

10PM Supper Club is a small app for a group of friends who go to late dinners in New York and rank them. Anyone can browse the club's dinners, and guests signed in to iCloud can add their own scores to dinners the club has rated.

Questions, problems, or something on a dinner's page that shouldn't be there? Email jojolom.lombardi@icloud.com. In the app, you can also tap Report a Problem from a dinner's menu.

Common questions

  • How do I join the club? Tap I'm in the Club, enter your name, and the club's founder will let you in. Membership is for the club's own members.
  • Why can't I score a dinner? Guests can score a dinner once the club has rated it, and need to be signed in to iCloud on their iPhone.
  • Do guests' scores change the club's ranking? No. They're shown separately, as a guest average.

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

What the app stores

Everything the app saves is kept in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), in the app's public database. Nothing is stored on any other server. What's saved:

  • Your iCloud user ID: an anonymous identifier Apple gives the app, so it knows which scores and rankings are yours. It isn't your Apple ID, email or phone number.
  • Your name, if you ask to join the club or are a member.
  • Members' content: dinners (restaurant, address, date, who was there), rankings, scores, notes, best dishes and photos.
  • Guests' scores for dinners the club has rated.

Who can see it

The club's dinners, rankings, members' names, notes, photos and scores, and the guest average, are visible to anyone using the app. A guest's individual scores are only shown combined into the guest average. Names of people asking to join are shown to the club's founder.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or passwords: the app uses the iCloud account already on your iPhone.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking, and no data is sold or shared with anyone.
  • No location tracking. Restaurant search uses Apple Maps around New York City.

Deleting your data

Members can delete dinners they've added from within the app. To have anything else removed, including your name, scores or notes, email jojolom.lombardi@icloud.com and it will be deleted.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new date.
